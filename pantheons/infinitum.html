The Pantheon of Infinitum

History

Before the dawn of creation, there existed only the Void, an endless, motionless entity encompassing everything and nothing. For countless millennia, it remained undisturbed until an event known as the Violent Split tore its existence apart over three hundred years.

The tremendous energies released during this cataclysm formed the Elemental Planes and the Planes of Positive and Negative Energy, which eventually converged to create the Elemental Chaos. From the remaining essence of the Void emerged Lathander, the God of Beginnings, the first divine consciousness.

For a millennium, Lathander shaped and governed the newly formed planes before dividing himself into six manifestations to share the burden of creation. However, disagreements between these manifestations escalated into the Dawn War, the first divine conflict. Although the war ended in a stalemate, each manifestation developed an independent identity, giving rise to the six original gods: Lathander, Melora, Akadi, Talos, Zehir, and Pelor.

They assumed dominion over Fire, Water, Air, Earth, Negative Energy, and Positive Energy respectively, establishing the first pantheon of Infinitum. Peace followed the Dawn War, but the growing harmony between the planes caused Positive and Negative Energy to converge, threatening the dominions of Pelor and Zehir.

Their resulting conflict, known as the War of the Elements, engulfed the Elemental Chaos and permanently transformed the universe. The violence displaced several gods from their original realms, prompting Lathander to establish Arcadia, Melora to create the Beastlands, and Talos to form Arborea, while Akadi successfully returned to the Plane of Air.

Fragments of elemental and divine energy scattered into the Astral Sea, combining to form the Ethereal Plane, Prime Material Plane, Feywild, and Shadowfell. As the war subsided, Zehir claimed the Elemental Plane of Fire, Akadi governed Air and Water, and Pelor assumed authority over Earth. The residual energies of the conflict also produced new divinities: Erathis, overseer of the Material Plane; Talona, born from Zehir's expended power and ruler of the Shadowfell; Eldath, daughter of Pelor and eventual ruler of the Feywild; and Lliira, a fairfolk deity who lost the Feywild to Eldath in a wager and subsequently created Bytopia.

With the universe expanding beyond its elemental origins, the gods gradually assumed authority over abstract concepts such as nature, renewal, law, malice, beauty, suffering, and emotion. When Lathander eventually created humanoid life, Zehir introduced malice into their nature, causing new moral and emotional domains to emerge. Unable to contain these conflicting aspects of mortality, Erathis divided, giving rise to Bhaal, god of murder and revenge, and Selune, goddess of desire and seduction.

As mortal civilizations developed, disputes over worship and divine influence initiated the Great Restructuring, a prolonged period of political maneuvering, limited warfare, divine births, and the redistribution of domains. Selune and Pelor became the first gods to conceive children together, producing Bahamut, who governed justice and mercy; Ilmater, who embodied suffering and perseverance; and Torm, who assumed loyalty, vitality, and healing.

Meanwhile, Zehir deceived and sexually assaulted Lliira, whose resulting trauma and vengeance caused her divine essence to divide, creating Cyric, god of sorrow, lies, crime, and despair. Outraged, Pelor and Bahamut waged war against Zehir, who received assistance from Talona. During the conflict, Pelor severed part of Zehir's leg, from which Avandra, goddess of trickery, emerged. With Lathander's assistance, Avandra deceived Zehir and stole his authority over the Plane of Fire, enabling Pelor to defeat him. Bahamut further mutilated Zehir, whose severed divine remains eventually produced Tiamat, goddess of greed and immorality, within the Astral Sea. Zehir retreated and established Hades as his new domain.

Elsewhere, Lathander and Melora married and gave birth to Ioun, creator of the Weave and goddess of arcane knowledge, and Kord, god of battle and strength. Their marriage also established divine authority over family, fertility, and childbirth, while Melora reclaimed the Plane of Water from Akadi. The union of Pelor and Eldath produced Diana, goddess of the moon and love, and Daedalus, god of architecture and craftsmanship. Eldath subsequently devoted herself to the Feywild and the arts, while Daedalus assisted Avandra in governing the Plane of Fire. The two eventually married, and Daedalus created Mechanus.

Toward the end of this era, an angel named Amos Deus led a rebellion of angels and devils against the gods, forcing them into a pact that granted him authority over contracts, punishment, and damnation. Renamed Asmodeus, he established the Nine Hells of Baator and its laws, bringing the Great Restructuring to an end with twenty-three established gods.

The conclusion of the Great Restructuring coincided with Lathander's awakening from his prolonged rest. Discovering that the pantheon had nearly doubled in size, he became consumed by paranoia, fearing that he had lost control of his creation. Drawing upon the primordial power of the Void remaining within him, Lathander seized every divine domain simultaneously, stripping the gods of their immortality and casting them into the Material Plane as mortals. This catastrophic event became known as the Dawn Cataclysm, a defining moment in the history and calendars of Infinitum.

Although diminished, Ioun's Weave survived, allowing the fallen gods to unite and launch an assault upon Arcadia. During the confrontation, Lathander killed Akadi, but the combined strength of the gods eventually overwhelmed him and restored their divine authority. Zehir attempted to exploit Akadi's death by claiming the Plane of Air, prompting Lathander to expend the last of his Void energy to imprison him within Hades. The resulting clash killed Talona, who sacrificed herself to protect Zehir, while the original participants of the Dawn War suffered lasting damage to their divine power.

In the aftermath, Auril, daughter of Daedalus and Avandra, inherited Akadi's dominion over the Plane of Air and its storms. With Talona dead and Zehir imprisoned, the Shadowfell remained vulnerable, allowing Vecna, a powerful lich, to absorb the souls of his followers and ascend to godhood, claiming undeath, secrets, deceit, and the Shadowfell.

Vecna's ascension alarmed Cyrila, a Fey Queen who sought to restore balance to the pantheon and prevent further divine conflicts. Assisted by Ioun, she attempted to ascend through a ritual powered by her followers, but interference from other ambitious wizards destabilized the process. The resulting vortex transported Cyrila and her city into the Shadowfell, where Vecna killed her. Yet from her corpse emerged the Raven Queen, whose completed ascension granted her authority over death, life, balance, and peace. She shattered Vecna's dominion over the Shadowfell, destroyed his works, and cast him into the Astral Sea. There, Vecna collided with remnants of the primordial Void, creating the infinite layers of the Abyss. Unable to reclaim the Shadowfell, he established himself upon the Abyss's first layer and created the demons, becoming their divine overseer.

Following the Dawn Cataclysm, the weakened pantheon entered a period of relative stability, increasingly relying upon mortal worshippers and champions to pursue divine ambitions rather than engaging in direct warfare. Approximately one year later, Talona returned as Varus, wounded and transformed, only to discover that her former dominion had been claimed. Retreating into the Astral Sea, Varus established Pandemonium, assuming authority over grief, madness, and the Underdark. Lathander likewise remained diminished, his former influence altered by the consequences of his paranoia and the loss of his primordial power.

By AC 300, the pantheon had settled into a fragile equilibrium, with the surviving gods governing their respective domains while new generations of divine beings emerged across the planes. The rise of lesser deities, demigods, and divine champions became increasingly prominent throughout Medeis, including Kalis, demigod and champion of Tiamat; Eyre, Goddess of Wraiths and lesser deity associated with Vecna; Rantus, the Maniac Demon and demigod of Varus; Umberlee, Goddess of Waves; and Raz, the Pale Berserker and champion of Varus. Their emergence demonstrated that divinity was no longer confined to the original gods or their direct descendants, but could be attained through mortal ascension, divine patronage, and the accumulation of extraordinary power. Thus, the history of the Infinitum Pantheon remains a chronicle of creation, conflict, betrayal, sacrifice, and transformation. From the Violent Split that birthed existence to the Dawn Cataclysm that nearly destroyed the divine order, each great upheaval reshaped the cosmos and redefined the nature of godhood. Though the great wars have subsided, the ambitions of the gods endure through their mortal followers, and the balance of the pantheon remains as uncertain as the primordial forces from which it was born.

The Pantheon

01. Lathander — The God of Beginnings

Divine Domains: Beginnings, Birth, Renewal, Self-Improvement, Paranoia, Marriage, Childbirth, Fertility

Planar Influence: Arcadia

Origin: The first divine consciousness to emerge from the primordial Void following the Violent Split.

Divine History: Lathander shaped the earliest planes for a millennium before dividing himself into six manifestations. Their growing independence sparked the Dawn War and established the original pantheon. After a prolonged rest, he awoke to find the divine order greatly expanded. Fearing the loss of his authority, he seized every divine domain using the remaining power of the Void, initiating the Dawn Cataclysm. During the gods' rebellion, he killed Akadi and later expended his final Void energy to imprison Zehir within Hades.

Relationships: Husband of Melora; father of Ioun and Kord. Primordial origin of the six original gods.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; diminished following the Dawn Cataclysm.

02. Melora — The Wild Mother

Divine Domains: Nature, Seasons, Wilderness, Beasts, Naval Voyages, Sailors, Seas

Planar Influence: Elemental Plane of Water; Beastlands

Origin: One of the six manifestations formed when Lathander divided his divine essence.

Divine History: Originally entrusted with the Elemental Plane of Water, Melora became an independent goddess following the Dawn War. During the War of the Elements, she was displaced from her original domain and created the Beastlands. She later married Lathander, forming the first divine marriage, and together they adopted the domains of family and fertility. She gave birth to Ioun and Kord and eventually relieved Akadi of the responsibility of governing two elemental planes by reclaiming Water.

Relationships: Wife of Lathander; mother of Ioun and Kord.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; retains influence over Water and the Beastlands.

03. Ioun — The Knowing Mistress

Divine Domains: Knowledge, Arcane Arts, Science, Sorcery, The Past, The Future, THE WEAVE

Planar Influence: No personal plane specified

Origin: Born to Lathander and Melora during the Great Restructuring.

Divine History: Ioun transformed the nature of magic by creating the Weave, a structure through which arcane power could be accessed and manipulated. Its existence allowed mortal spellcasters to develop more sophisticated forms of sorcery and magical study. During the Dawn Cataclysm, the Weave continued to exist in a diminished form even after Lathander stripped the gods of their divinity, helping the fallen gods resist him. Ioun later assisted Cyrila in preparing the ritual intended to elevate the Fey Queen to divinity.

Relationships: Daughter of Lathander and Melora; sister of Kord; assisted Cyrila.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; creator and owner of the Weave.

04. Kord — The God of Battle

Divine Domains: Battle, War, Brutality, Equality, Strength, Weapons

Planar Influence: No personal plane specified

Origin: Son of Lathander and Melora, born during the Great Restructuring.

Divine History: Kord's youth was shaped by the violent struggle between Pelor and Zehir. The conflict influenced the development of his divine identity, linking him to warfare, physical strength, brutality, and the instruments of battle. Unlike deities whose powers originated from the elemental planes, Kord's authority reflects the growing importance of combat and martial struggle in the developing universe. He remained among the established gods following the Dawn Cataclysm.

Relationships: Son of Lathander and Melora; brother of Ioun.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; retains his martial domains.

05. Auril — The Goddess of Storms

Divine Domains: Winged Beasts, Storms, Hurricanes, Thunder, Lightning, Winter

Planar Influence: Elemental Plane of Air

Origin: Daughter of Avandra and Daedalus, born following the Dawn Cataclysm.

Divine History: Auril emerged after the death of Akadi, one of the six original gods, during Lathander's attempt to reclaim control of the pantheon. Akadi's death left the Elemental Plane of Air without its ancient overseer. Auril inherited the vacant plane and the domains previously associated with its fallen ruler, including storms, thunder, lightning, winter, and winged beasts. Her succession restored divine governance to the plane and marked the replacement of a primordial deity by a younger divine generation.

Relationships: Daughter of Avandra and Daedalus; successor to Akadi.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; overseer of the Elemental Plane of Air.

06. Talos — The God of Vigilance

Divine Domains: Vigilance, Persistence, Intent, Bravery, Hard Work

Planar Influence: Arborea

Origin: One of the six original manifestations of Lathander, originally governing the Elemental Plane of Earth.

Divine History: Talos became an independent deity following the Dawn War. During the War of the Elements, the conflict between Pelor and Zehir displaced him from his original plane, leaving him unable to return. He subsequently created Arborea as his new realm. His domains evolved over time, moving from broader associations with war and health toward vigilance, persistence, determination, and hard work. As one of the original participants in the Dawn War, his divinity was damaged by the exhaustion of the Void's remaining power during the Dawn Cataclysm.

Relationships: One of the six original gods; shared primordial origin with Lathander, Melora, Akadi, Zehir, and Pelor.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; divine power damaged during the Cataclysm.

07. Pelor — The Dawn Father

Divine Domains: Light, Sun, Kindness, Law, Order

Planar Influence: Elemental Plane of Earth

Origin: One of the six original gods, initially overseeing the Plane of Positive Energy.

Divine History: Pelor's conflict with Zehir initiated the War of the Elements, after which he assumed control of the Elemental Plane of Earth. During the Great Restructuring, he fathered Bahamut, Ilmater, and Torm with Selune, and Diana and Daedalus with Eldath. Following Zehir's assault on Lliira, Pelor joined Bahamut in attacking him, severing part of his leg and eventually defeating him after Avandra deprived him of Fire. Pelor survived the Dawn Cataclysm but suffered damage to his ancient divinity.

Relationships: Father of Bahamut, Ilmater, Torm, Diana, and Daedalus; longstanding enemy of Zehir.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; one of the original gods weakened by the Cataclysm.

08. Eldath — The Goddess of the Fey

Divine Domains: Time, Fey, Craftsmanship, Design, Beauty, Art, Poetry, Music

Planar Influence: Feywild

Origin: Daughter of Pelor, born when his divine force struck the newly formed Feywild.

Divine History: Eldath became associated with the Feywild during the War of the Elements. Although Lliira initially possessed the realm, Eldath won control through a wager, prompting Lliira's departure to Bytopia. Eldath originally held domains associated with love, daylight, beauty, and twilight. Her relationship with Pelor produced Diana, who inherited many of these earlier responsibilities, and Daedalus, who assumed the crafts of construction and smithing. Eldath consequently concentrated her divine attention on time, the fey, design, art, poetry, and music.

Relationships: Daughter of Pelor; mother of Diana and Daedalus; former rival of Lliira over the Feywild.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; overseer of the Feywild.

09. Diana — The Goddess of the Moon

Divine Domains: Moon, Love, Youth, Courting, Longing, Beauty, Night, Twilight

Planar Influence: No personal plane specified

Origin: Daughter of Pelor and Eldath.

Divine History: Diana emerged from the relationship between Pelor and Eldath during the Great Restructuring. Her birth accompanied a major redistribution of Eldath's divine responsibilities. As Eldath devoted herself more fully to the Feywild, craftsmanship, and artistic expression, Diana inherited many of her mother's former domains. She became associated with the moon, romance, youth, longing, night, and twilight, representing aspects of divine influence that had previously belonged to Eldath.

Relationships: Daughter of Pelor and Eldath; sister of Daedalus.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; retains her celestial and romantic domains.

10. Daedalus — The God of Craftsmanship

Divine Domains: Architecture, Crafting, Smithing, Building

Planar Influence: Elemental Plane of Fire; Mechanus

Origin: Son of Pelor and Eldath, born during the Great Restructuring.

Divine History: Daedalus emerged when Eldath shifted her divine attention toward the Feywild and the arts. He inherited responsibility for architecture, smithing, building, and practical craftsmanship. When Avandra struggled to govern the Elemental Plane of Fire, she sought his assistance. Their cooperation developed into love, and the two married, sharing authority over Fire. Daedalus subsequently created Mechanus and became its overseer. Following the death of Akadi during the Dawn Cataclysm, Daedalus and Avandra's daughter Auril inherited the Plane of Air.

Relationships: Son of Pelor and Eldath; brother of Diana; husband of Avandra; father of Auril.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; oversees Mechanus and shares Fire with Avandra.

11. Selune — The Goddess of Desire

Divine Domains: Lust, Desire, Youth, Sex, Seduction, Envy, Jealousy, Torture

Planar Influence: No personal plane specified

Origin: Emerged when Erathis divided under the growing burden of mortal domains.

Divine History: After Lathander created humanoids and Zehir introduced malice into their nature, the resulting complexity of mortal existence overwhelmed Erathis. Her divine essence split, producing Selune and Bhaal. Selune inherited domains connected to desire, seduction, envy, and jealousy. During the Great Restructuring, she and Pelor became the first gods to conceive children together. Their offspring remained within her until suitable domains became available, eventually emerging as Bahamut, Ilmater, and Torm.

Relationships: Emerged from Erathis; connected to Bhaal through their shared origin; mother of Bahamut, Ilmater, and Torm.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; retains her domains of desire and related emotions.

12. Bahamut — The God of Justice

Divine Domains: Justice, Mercy, Punishment, Forgiveness, Wisdom

Planar Influence: No personal plane specified

Origin: The first child of Selune and Pelor to emerge during the Great Restructuring.

Divine History: Bahamut remained within Selune until the concepts necessary for his divine existence had developed among mortals. He emerged as the god of justice, mercy, punishment, forgiveness, and wisdom. Following Zehir's violation of Lliira, Bahamut joined Pelor in declaring war against him. After Zehir lost his authority over Fire and was defeated, Bahamut inflicted a severe punishment upon him, severing his loins and casting them into the Astral Sea. These remains later contributed to Tiamat's emergence.

Relationships: Son of Pelor and Selune; brother of Ilmater and Torm; opponent of Zehir.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; retains his domains of justice and mercy.

13. Ilmater — The God of Suffering

Divine Domains: Suffering, Martyrdom, Perseverance, Endurance

Planar Influence: No personal plane specified

Origin: Child of Pelor and Selune, emerging after Bahamut during the Great Restructuring.

Divine History: Ilmater was conceived alongside his siblings but did not immediately enter the world. Like the other children of Selune and Pelor, he remained unborn until the development of mortal existence produced domains appropriate to his divine identity. He emerged to govern suffering, martyrdom, perseverance, and endurance. He survived the Dawn Cataclysm and retained his place among the established gods.

Relationships: Son of Pelor and Selune; brother of Bahamut and Torm.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; retains his domains.

14. Torm — The God of Loyalty

Divine Domains: Loyalty, Surrender, Vitality, Healing, War, Prisoners

Planar Influence: No personal plane specified

Origin: The last of Pelor and Selune's three children to emerge during the Great Restructuring.

Divine History: Torm's birth completed the emergence of the three divine children conceived by Selune and Pelor. His existence was tied to the development of mortal ideas surrounding loyalty, surrender, health, warfare, and captivity. He assumed responsibility for vitality, healing, and prisoners alongside his other domains. Unlike several older gods, whose authority originated from elemental forces or divine conflict, Torm's identity developed directly from the social and moral complexities of mortal civilization.

Relationships: Son of Pelor and Selune; brother of Bahamut and Ilmater.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; retains his domains.

15. Avandra — The Goddess of Trickery

Divine Domains: Trickery, Trade, Decisions, Keys, Doors, Travellers, Destinations

Planar Influence: Elemental Plane of Fire

Origin: Born from a fragment of Zehir's leg severed by Pelor during their conflict.

Divine History: Avandra arose when a portion of Zehir's divine flesh was cast into the Astral Sea. With assistance from the largely inactive Lathander, she exploited Zehir's trust and stole his authority over the Elemental Plane of Fire, transferring it to Pelor. Following Zehir's defeat, she received the plane as a reward. Finding its governance difficult, she enlisted Daedalus, with whom she eventually fell in love and married. Their daughter Auril later succeeded Akadi as overseer of the Plane of Air.

Relationships: Originated from Zehir's severed divine essence; wife of Daedalus; mother of Auril.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; shares the Plane of Fire with Daedalus.

16. Erathis — The Goddess of Mortality

Divine Domains: Chastity, Purity, Mortality

Planar Influence: Prime Material Plane

Origin: Formed from divine energy released during the War of the Elements.

Divine History: Erathis emerged alongside the formation of the Prime Material Plane and became its divine overseer. When Lathander created humanoids and Zehir introduced malice into their nature, the growing range of mortal emotions and behaviors generated more domains than Erathis could govern. The strain caused her divine essence to divide, producing Selune and Bhaal. Erathis retained her position over the Material Plane while concentrating on chastity, purity, and mortality. She remained part of the pantheon following the Dawn Cataclysm.

Relationships: Divine source from which Selune and Bhaal emerged.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; oversees the Material Plane.

17. Lliira — The Goddess of Joy

Divine Domains: Joy, Laughter, Harmony

Planar Influence: Bytopia

Origin: A fairfolk deity born during the formation of the Feywild.

Divine History: Lliira initially possessed the Feywild but lost it to Eldath through a wager. She withdrew into the Astral Sea and created Bytopia, where she governed opposing emotional domains including joy and sorrow, laughter and grief, harmony and dissonance. During the Great Restructuring, Zehir attempted to court her and, after being rejected, deceived and sexually assaulted her. The resulting trauma and vengeance overwhelmed her divine nature, causing her to divide into Lliira and Cyric. Lliira retained joy, laughter, and harmony, while Cyric inherited her darker emotional domains.

Relationships: Former rival of Eldath; victim of Zehir; divine origin of Cyric.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; governs Bytopia and her remaining emotional domains.

18. Cyric — The God of Sorrow

Divine Domains: Sorrow, Dissonance, Lies, Crime, Despair

Planar Influence: No personal plane specified

Origin: Emerged when Lliira's divine essence divided following her traumatic encounter with Zehir.

Divine History: Cyric was not born through conventional divine parentage or the residual energies of warfare. His existence resulted from Lliira's inability to contain the suffering and vengeance caused by Zehir's actions. Her divine nature separated into two beings, with Cyric inheriting sorrow, dissonance, lies, crime, and despair. His emergence permanently separated these domains from Lliira's remaining authority over joy, laughter, and harmony. He continued to exist as an independent member of the pantheon after the Great Restructuring and Dawn Cataclysm.

Relationships: Originated from Lliira's divided divine essence.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; independent deity.

19. Zehir — The God of Malice

Divine Domains: Malice, Darkness, Despair, Trickery, Madness, Destruction

Planar Influence: Hades

Origin: One of the six original gods, initially ruling the Plane of Negative Energy.

Divine History: Zehir's conflict with Pelor initiated the War of the Elements, after which he claimed the Elemental Plane of Fire. He later introduced malice into Lathander's humanoid creations. During the Great Restructuring, his assault on Lliira provoked Pelor and Bahamut to attack him. Avandra deprived him of Fire, and his defeat led him to establish Hades. During the Dawn Cataclysm, he attempted to seize the Plane of Air after Akadi's death. Lathander used his remaining Void power to imprison Zehir within Hades, and Talona died while protecting him from the resulting clash.

Relationships: Primordial counterpart and enemy of Pelor; source of Avandra and Tiamat; protected by Talona.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; imprisoned within Hades.

20. Tiamat — The Goddess of Greed

Divine Domains: Greed, Trickery, Immorality, Degeneracy

Planar Influence: No personal plane specified

Origin: Emerged from Zehir's severed divine remains after they were consumed by the Astral Sea.

Divine History: Following Zehir's defeat by Pelor and Bahamut, part of his divine body was cast into the Astral Sea. Its interaction with the surrounding astral energies produced Tiamat, a new goddess whose domains reflected greed, trickery, immorality, and degeneracy. She entered the pantheon during the Great Restructuring and remained among the principal gods after the Dawn Cataclysm. By AC 300, her influence was also represented through Kalis, a demigod identified as her champion.

Relationships: Originated from Zehir's severed divine essence; patron of Kalis.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; established member of the pantheon.

21. Vecna — The Lord of the Abyss

Divine Domains: Demons, Undeath, Lies, Secrets, Deceit

Planar Influence: Abyss

Origin: Originally a lich who ascended to divinity by absorbing the souls of his followers.

Divine History: Vecna exploited the death of Talona and Zehir's imprisonment during the Dawn Cataclysm to claim the vacant Shadowfell. He ascended by consuming his followers' souls while the weakened gods were unable to intervene. His rise prompted Cyrila to seek divinity, but when her ritual collapsed and transported her city into the Shadowfell, Vecna killed her. Cyrila subsequently rose as the Raven Queen, shattered his works, and cast him into the Astral Sea. There, his collision with remnants of the primordial Void created the infinite layers of the Abyss. Vecna established himself upon its first layer and created the demons.

Relationships: Enemy of the Raven Queen; associated with Eyre, Goddess of Wraiths.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; oversees the Abyss.

22. Bhaal — The God of Murder

Divine Domains: Murder, Revenge, Greed, Pride, Suicide, Sickness

Planar Influence: No personal plane specified

Origin: Emerged from the division of Erathis following the creation and moral development of humanoids.

Divine History: When Lathander created humanoid life, Zehir introduced malice into the developing mortals. The resulting conflict of emotions, impulses, and behaviors generated divine responsibilities beyond Erathis's capacity. Her essence divided, producing Bhaal and Selune. Bhaal assumed the destructive aspects of mortal existence, including murder, revenge, greed, pride, suicide, and sickness. He became an established member of the pantheon during the Great Restructuring and retained his domains after the Dawn Cataclysm.

Relationships: Emerged from Erathis alongside Selune.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; retains his destructive mortal domains.

23. Cyrila — The Raven Queen

Divine Domains: Death, Life, Balance, Peace

Planar Influence: Shadowfell

Origin: Originally a Fey Queen who sought divine ascension to restore balance within the pantheon.

Divine History: After Vecna seized the Shadowfell, Cyrila recognized the danger of unchecked evil and sought to become a mediator among the gods. With Ioun's assistance, she prepared an ascension ritual powered by her followers. Other wizards attempted to siphon the gathered energy, and Cyrila's efforts to banish them destabilized the ritual. A vortex transported her and her city into the Shadowfell, where Vecna killed her. From her corpse rose the Raven Queen, who shattered Vecna's dominion, cast him into the Astral Sea, and claimed the Shadowfell along with the domains of death, life, balance, and peace.

Relationships: Assisted by Ioun; enemy and successor to Vecna in the Shadowfell.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; overseer of the Shadowfell.

24. Asmodeus — The Lord of the Nine Hells

Divine Domains: Deals, Contracts, Dues, Punishment, Damnation, Devils

Planar Influence: Nine Hells of Baator

Origin: Originally Amos Deus, an angel who rebelled against the gods during the Great Restructuring.

Divine History: As divine conflicts continued, angels became increasingly resentful of being used as proxies in the gods' wars. Amos Deus organized a rebellion involving angels and devils, assembling a force powerful enough to bring several gods close to oblivion. Rather than continue the conflict, the gods negotiated a pact granting him authority over contracts, dues, punishment, damnation, and devils. He established the Nine Hells to receive souls bound by duty and contractual obligations. The devils migrated there, the Law of Baator was established, and Amos Deus entered the pantheon as Asmodeus.

Relationships: Former leader of a rebellion against the gods; ruler of the devils.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Surviving; oversees the Nine Hells.

25. Varus — The Lord of Pandemonium

Divine Domains: Grief, Madness, Underdark

Planar Influence: Pandemonium

Origin: The returned identity of Talona, originally born from Zehir's residual divine energy.

Divine History: As Talona, this deity emerged during the War of the Elements and became overseer of the Shadowfell, governing pain, punishment, death, deceit, and chaos. She assisted Zehir during his conflict with Pelor and Bahamut. During the Dawn Cataclysm, Talona sacrificed herself to protect Zehir from Lathander's attack and was believed dead. Approximately one year later, she returned as Varus, wounded and broken. Finding the Shadowfell transformed and occupied by the Raven Queen, Varus withdrew into the Astral Sea and created Pandemonium, assuming authority over grief, madness, and the Underdark.

Relationships: Originated from Zehir's power; sacrificed herself to protect him; associated with Rantus and Raz.

Post-Cataclysm Status: Returned after death; oversees Pandemonium.

Lesser Deities, Demigods, and Champions — AC 300

26. Kalis — Demigod and Champion of Tiamat

Divine Domains: Not specified

History: Kalis is identified as a demigod and champion of Tiamat among the prominent lesser divine figures who had risen by AC 300. The mythology establishes the connection to Tiamat but does not yet describe Kalis's origins, individual powers, or major exploits.

27. Eyre — The Goddess of Wraiths

Divine Domains: Wraiths

History: Eyre is a lesser deity associated with Vecna, appearing among the notable divine figures of the post-Cataclysm era. Her title establishes her connection to wraiths, but the supplied mythology does not provide a full origin or account of her ascension.
